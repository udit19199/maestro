About Elip
Good matches have reasons. A title leaves things out: "more responsibility" can mean leading a team, or owning the work without managing anyone. Elip asks people what they want to do next, reads millions of jobs each morning with that in mind, and sends the ones a friend who knows them would send, each with why. Companies add a role and get a short list of people who want that kind of work, each with the reason. Elip is backed by Y Combinator (W25).
Who you'd work with
Elip was founded by Gaurav Luhariwala. Before Elip he built and scaled data-driven products for banks and financial institutions. We are a team of four, so you work directly with the founder and the engineers, and what you build goes to real users.
About the role
A six-month Software Engineering internship with a pre-placement offer (PPO) opportunity. You would work on the part of Elip that decides what is worth sending: understanding what a person wants from a conversation, finding the roles that fit among millions of live job postings re-read every morning, and saying why in plain words.
Your first 30, 60 and 90 days
We are four people, so there is no ticket queue and no shadowing period. You own things from the first month.
- Week 1: your first change is live. You read real conversations and real matches, find one place where Elip sent the wrong job or gave a weak reason, and fix it
- By day 30: you own a feature end to end. For example, the reason Elip writes beside every match, or how it remembers what a person said they would never take. You decide what to build, ship it, and prove it works with an eval you wrote
- By day 60: you own a whole vertical of the product. One of: the conversation that learns what a person wants, the morning read of millions of job postings, the ranking that decides what gets sent, or the short list a company gets for a role. Its quality is your number, and you are the person we ask when it moves
- By day 90: you work forward-deployed. You sit with the companies hiring through Elip and the people it is finding work for, hear where a short list or a match missed, and ship the fix that week. From the call to the code, with no hand-off
- At six months: a PPO decision based on what you owned and shipped

About the interview
Step 1, application: apply through the Elip link in the job description. Every application is read, and we shortlist from the resume and the answers you give there. No lists or referrals are needed.
Step 2, technical interview: a conversation on Google Meet about something you have built, how you would approach matching people to jobs, and your hands-on work with LLMs, retrieval or backend systems. There is no written or aptitude test and no group discussion.
Step 3, HR and founder conversation: a short call on Google Meet with the founder about what you want from the internship, how you work, and practical details such as start date and location.
In some cases we add one more interview. In total there are two to three rounds, all online. Selected interns receive a formal internship offer, and we tell every shortlisted candidate the outcome.
