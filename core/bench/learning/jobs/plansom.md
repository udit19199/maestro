About Plansom
Plansom is building technology that turns everyday AI into measured business results. Most businesses are already using AI, but few have embedded it into how they operate. Making individual tasks faster does not necessarily make the business faster.
We believe AI should change how a company works, not just its tools. Plansom makes redesigning business workflows around AI simple and measurable. Our platform enables teams to set business goals, redesign workflows, bring people and AI agents together and measure the results.
Our approach is simple: set the goal, get it done, prove the result. Plansom was featured on the Microsoft Blog for building the Plansom Agent for Microsoft Teams, powered by Microsoft Foundry SDKs and Azure OpenAI. We are part of the Microsoft startup programme and work closely with Microsoft as we continue to build and scale.
We are early, but deliberate - live product, real users, weekly releases and a clear path to scale.
