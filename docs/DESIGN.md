# Design

Follow the supplied Grok Bot screenshots for the two-pane messaging layout, bot avatars, conversation previews, work cards, and rounded bottom composer. Dots is a reference for assigning responsibilities and reviewing work.

Keep the sidebar visible while Settings occupies the right pane. Providers and MCP servers are separate lists, with connection actions on their rows. Do not open a separate Settings window.

Only show work and capabilities that are actually available. Bot creation and secondary integrations are not part of this step.
